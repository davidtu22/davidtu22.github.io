I.

Abstract

I spent four years being trained to interrogate the institutions I'm now trying to join — reading about platform incentives and extraction on a Tuesday, drafting a funding proposal on a Thursday. The College of Social Studies at Wesleyan doesn't let you separate economic analysis from its consequences, and I don't think I want to un-learn that going into tech.

So I'll say it plainly: I have not swallowed the pill. I'm probably still a cynic about a lot of what Silicon Valley tells itself. But I also spent two years turning a public university's chaotic budget process into something 150+ student organizations could actually plan around, which is closer to product work than either an econ department or a tech company would usually admit. I'd rather bring the critique in than pretend I've left it at the door.

Translation: I have four years of opinions about Silicon Valley and I'm about to go ask it for a job.
